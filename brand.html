The name

The part of a shadow where the light is gone completely.

In astronomy a shadow has two regions. The umbra is the core, where the light source is entirely blocked. Around it lies the penumbra, where the blocking is partial and some light still falls.

Melanism in leachianus is a simple recessive. An animal either shows it or carries it. The vocabulary already existed — it just happened to be sitting in a different discipline.

In Chinese the same two regions are 本影 and 半影, which is what the genetics gets called here. The house name is 玄影 — 玄 being the old word for a black so deep it reads as unfathomable rather than merely dark.

The mark

A pupil in total darkness.

Nocturnal geckos do not have round pupils. In light the slit closes into a vertical chain of separate pinholes, each projecting its own image onto the retina. It is the most specific thing about how this animal looks at you, and almost nobody draws it.

The iris is drawn as fine radial fibres of uneven length, the way a leachianus iris is actually veined. A symmetrical starburst would have been decoration.
